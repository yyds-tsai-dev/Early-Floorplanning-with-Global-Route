**Venue sweeps (mostly negative — valuable as closure):**

- **ISPD 2026** (14 papers, DBLP TOC read in full): only GrandPlan (known) and the soft-module ILP paper (A4 below) are relevant.
- **ASP-DAC 2026 / DATE 2026 / ICCAD 2025**: zero applicable floorplanning papers (only 3D-IC/chiplet/RL items; ICCAD 2025 TOC has literally no "floorplan" hit). DBLP TOC queries cited in the sources list.
- **FTAFP and Piano still have no public code**; Piano remains a v1 preprint with no venue and no successor from the USTC/Huawei group.
- **"F-LG"** (a name floated in the survey brief): could not be resolved to any primary source via DBLP/arXiv/web — treated as a loose reference to the analytical global-floorplanning + legalization family (PeF-style), which stays in the rejected-as-engine class.

**A1. Topology-frozen convex coordinate re-solve (WL polish post-pass)** — *Yimamu, Pastor-Rué, Cortadella (UPC), "Chip Floorplanning Combining Convex and Non-convex Optimization", arXiv:2607.21408 (2026-07-23)*, `https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21408`. Mechanism: three-phase framework whose transferable piece is the **legalization phase — a log-transformed convex model over the H/V constraint graphs** that re-solves block coordinates (and sizes: log makes `w·h ≥ A` convex) minimizing WL; ≥1–5% HPWL gains vs SOTA on MCNC/GSRC/HB+. Why: attacks **α·WL** (45–63% of our cost) + OA secondarily, on the geometry SA leaves quantized. Sketch: freeze the best placement's topology, extract constraint graphs, minimize guiding-point-tracking WL over x/y (+ soft w/h in log domain) under non-overlap/outline/AR/LOCATION; re-route, exact-score gate, anytime-safe. Effort M; risk: channel re-slicing shifts routes/overflow — trust-region + accept-only-if-better. **PRIOR-variant** (GP/SOCP lineage with fresh 2026 evidence and a concrete constraint-graph formulation; converges with §4-C1 below).

**A2. Quadratic-connectivity seeding of restarts** — same paper, phase 1. Mechanism: cheap quadratic placement (blocks as points, Σw·dist², overlaps allowed) as a topology-aware start with measurable final-WL benefit. Why: our portfolio restarts are generic; a connectivity-clustered seed biases every leg toward low-WL basins; closed-form at n≤24. Sketch: solve the 2n-variable quadratic system per restart (edge blocks pinned as boundary conditions), order blocks by x/y → initial sequence pair via the existing `seedFromPlacement`. Effort S; risk low (worst case: one more seed in the sweep). **PRIOR-variant** (deep_research_report_gpt_v2.md already floated "analytical as seed generator"; this supplies the 2026 evidence + recipe).

**A3. Constructive beam-search leg** — *Luo, Zhao, Li, Su, Zhang, Ding, Lü (HUST), "A reduction framework with an improved beam search algorithm for non-slicing VLSI floorplanning", J. Supercomputing 82:402 (2026-05-08)*, `https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11227-026-08533-2`. Mechanism: reformulates floorplanning as a series of fixed-width subproblems, each solved by beam search whose constructive scoring mixes area + WL (details paywalled). Why: a deterministic constructive search is maximally diverse vs our 8 SA legs → new basins for **α·WL/OA**. Sketch: beam over block insertions into the B\*-contour, beam-scored by `cheapScore`, run as a portfolio leg or seed generator. Effort M–L; risk: reimplementation from abstract, unproven on routed-WL. **NOVEL**.

**A4. Discrete soft-block shape planning via exact optimization** — *Song, Phan, Huang, Fang (NTUST) + Synopsys, "Technology-Aware 3D Placement with ILP-Based Region Planning for Soft Modules", ISPD 2026*, `https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3764386.3779584`. Mechanism: an ILP picks the optimal region/shape per soft module; **shape flexibility alone yields >21% WL reduction** in their flow. Why: fresh, quantified evidence that *joint* soft-shape selection (vs our local ±AR mutations) is worth double-digit WL; also lets shapes pre-reserve FT inflation area. Sketch: at final-elite polish, enumerate k discretized ARs per soft block and solve the joint selection exactly (tiny DP/B&B over the constraint graph at n≤24) minimizing guiding-point WL; exact-score gate. Effort M; risk: shape changes invalidate cached routes; L-shape part does not transfer. **PRIOR-variant** (Stockmeyer/Lagrangian/SDS lineage; the *exact joint discrete selection* + 2026 evidence is the new part).

**A5. "Human-likeness" whitespace regularizers for the cheap tier** — *Kahng, Kundu, Pramanik (UCSD), "MAGE: Human-Like Macro Placement via Agentic Multimodal Reasoning", arXiv:2607.18536 (July 2026)*, `https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18536`. Mechanism: defines four quantitative metrics experts optimize that PPA proxies miss — **notch, whitespace, pocket, alignment scores** — and shows optimizing them helps downstream quality at comparable WL. Why: our channels ARE whitespace slices; fragmented/notched whitespace → bad channel extraction → longer guiding-point chains + overflow. The cheap tier is currently blind to channel *shape* (measured τ 0.067–0.807, pool_τ sometimes negative — b6-report). Sketch: O(n log n) sweep-line computation of the four scores per decode; weight-swept as a flag variant. Effort S–M; risk: proxy mismatch — flag-gate + τ-harness. **NOVEL** (metric definitions 2026; ignore the LLM machinery around them).

## 3. Thread B — WL-focused routing, incremental exact eval, congestion proxies

**Negative findings first**: no D\*-Lite/dynamic-shortest-path literature in EDA routing (solution reuse, not search-tree reuse, is the evidenced mechanism); no study showing any static net order (incl. our heaviest-first) is reliably best; no published rip-up round-budget study at small scale; 2020–2026 congestion-estimation literature for floorplanning is ML-image-model dominated (wrong scale for n=7–24).

**B1. Constant cost-gap negotiation pricing** — *Zha & Li, "Revisiting PathFinder Routing Algorithm", FPGA 2022, DOI 10.1145/3490422.3502356*, `https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3490422.3502356`. Mechanism: shows PathFinder quality is order-dependent (VTR's sorted order suboptimal on 14/18 benchmarks) *because* of the exponentially-escalating present-cost gap; redefines congestion costs so a **constant, correctly-sized gap** suffices — more convergence per round, order sensitivity largely removed. Why: with our hard 3-round cap, an escalating gap either under-prices (residual overflow) or over-prices (WL detours) depending on bundle order → targets the **overflow↔WL balance**. Sketch: replace round-indexed escalation with a fixed gap sized from the official 20·A·R term (one unit of overuse must out-price the worst legal detour); keep tail-regime pricing as tie-breaker. Effort S; risk low (flag-gate + A/B). **NOVEL** (directly on-point evidence absent from corpus).

**B2. ECO-style warm-start exact tier (dirty-bundle re-route + carried history)** — *Starfish (ICCAD 2021)* `https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9643517/`; *CRoute (FCCM 2019, 11% WL / 3.4× faster by ripping up only illegal connections)* `https://biblio.ugent.be/publication/8640095/file/8640096.pdf`; *congestion-history preload cuts PathFinder iterations 43% (MLCAD 2022)* `https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3551901.3556492`. Mechanism: after a local placement change, keep the previous routing, re-route only bundles that are dirty (incident to moved blocks / unmappable channels), seed negotiation history from the prior solve. Why: **exact-tier throughput is the binding constraint** (case4 fits only ~55k iterations in 600 s); most bundles are clean after one SA move → plausibly 2–5× cheaper exact calls → proportionally more exact evals. Sketch: persist `RoutingResult`; geometrically match new↔old channels by strip x-range + y-interval overlap; re-route dirty bundles with warm history; one cleanup round if overflow appears; final accepted best always re-scored from scratch (self-consistency suite is the guard). Effort M–L (channel identity across placements is the hard part); risk medium (incremental/from-scratch drift). **NOVEL**.

**B3. WL recovery pass: candidate-path set + joint reassignment** — *GRIP (DAC 2009)* `https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1629911.1629999`; *ILPGRC (TCAD 2023)* `https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10218743/`. Mechanism: enumerate k candidate paths per net, then jointly select one per net under capacity constraints (ILP), removing sequential-ordering artifacts. Why: **α·WL** — sequential Dijkstra locks early bundles into detours; our instance (≤~60 merged bundles, <150-node graph) is tiny enough for exact or near-exact selection. Sketch: after negotiation, Yen-style k diverse shortest paths per bundle costed by true guiding-point WL; hand-rolled B&B or (S variant) iterated one-bundle best-response reassignment holding others fixed. Effort M (S for the local-reassignment variant); risk medium — FT tiering makes the ILP non-pure-linear; no external solver in a static contest binary. **PRIOR-variant** (distinct from the rejected fractional MCMF/Lagrangian: integral explicit-path selection).

**B4. L/Z bend-feasibility as a cheap-tier WL term** — *Law & Young, "Multi-bend bus driven floorplanning" (ISPD 2005 / Integration 2008)* `http://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/~fyyoung/paper/integration08_bus.pdf`; *Ma & Young, TCG-based (ASP-DAC 2008)*. Mechanism: bus-driven floorplanning evaluates, inside every SA move, whether a block-pair bus is realizable with 0/1/2 bends via constant-time representation checks — i.e., L/Z pattern routing *without a router*. Why: the scored WL is a guiding-point chain that punishes bends/detours HPWL cannot see → closes the cheap↔exact gap on the heaviest bundles. Sketch: in `cheapScore`, for top-m heaviest bundles compute an L/Z realizability bit from rectangle visibility (shared interval along x then y), charge `α·est_detour` when only bent paths exist; O(m)/move. Effort S–M; risk low-medium (calibrate on cached exact pairs). **PRIOR-variant** (BDF known only as a passing mention; the proxy repurposing is new).

**B5. Channel local-density lower bound as O(bundles) overflow predictor** — *Yoshimura & Kuh, "Efficient Algorithms for Channel Routing", IEEE TCAD CAD-1:25–35 (1982)* (density = max interval-stabbing count, a *lower bound* on required tracks); floorplanning-congestion precedent *Wong & Young, ASP-DAC 2003*. Mechanism: project each bundle's plausible crossing interval onto each channel axis; max stabbing count vs `25·length` is a router-free per-channel overflow veto with a guarantee RUDY lacks. Sketch: sorted-endpoint sweep per cheap eval, charge the official 20·A·R shape. Effort S; risk low (it is a lower bound — that is the point). **PRIOR-variant** (classic, unported to this niche).

**B6. FTAFP feedthrough-estimation skeleton for the cheap FT proxy** — *FTAFP, ASP-DAC 2025, DOI 10.1145/3658617.3697728*, `https://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/~byu/papers/C245-ASPDAC2025-FTAFP.pdf`. Mechanism: predicts which modules get transited and how many nets without routing (line-of-sight between src/dst over block rectangles), so SA avoids placements only the exact tier currently rejects. **PRIOR** (FTAFP long known; included only as the concrete estimator recipe for a cheap-tier port; effort M, risk medium).

## 4. Thread C — exact small-n methods, WL-driven whitespace redistribution, alignment

**Headline**: the threads converge on one family — **Bonn's exact WL-optimal relative-placement B&B whose inner bound is an LP/network-simplex over the constraint graph**, and **Tang/Tian/Wong's proof that fixed-topology WL minimization is a min-cost flow**. Our respace machinery is one objective swap away.

**C1. Min-cost-flow whitespace redistribution for WL** — *Tang, Tian, Wong, "Minimizing wire length in floorplanning", IEEE TCAD 25(9):1744–1753 (2006); conference version ASP-DAC 2005 pp.412–417*, `https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6e817b145bd99c524a78b0e64d68120e8d74e931`, `https://research.ibm.com/publications/minimizing-wire-length-in-floorplanning`. Mechanism: for a **fixed topology**, minimizing total weighted HPWL over block positions is an LP (|xi−xj| via per-net min/max bound variables) whose **dual is a min-cost network flow** — polynomial, optimal, and it natively supports fixed frame, pre-placed/boundary blocks, range placement, **alignment and abutment** constraints "without loss of optimality". Reported **4.2% average WL improvement on already-compact floorplans**. Why: direct attack on the dominant **α·WL** term; the 2023-D winner's cheap whitespace-redistribution phase (2026-07-23 doc §6) is the heuristic cousin — the corpus never had the principled formulation. Sketch: reuse the P3/B7 constraint-graph machinery per elite; solve flow-based x then y redistribution with bundle weights (or first cut: iterated weighted-median sweeps under longest-path slack bounds); exact-score gate absorbs the HPWL↔guiding-point mismatch. Effort M (~300 LOC MCMF or dual-ascent on existing machinery); risk low-medium. **NOVEL** (mechanism absent from corpus; P3 respace is overflow-driven, B7 is capacity-profit-driven).

**C2. Exact WL-optimal placement B&B at our n** — *Funke, Hougardy, Schneider, "An exact algorithm for wirelength optimal placements in VLSI design", Integration 52:355–366 (2016)*, `http://www.or.uni-bonn.de/~hougardy/paper/ExactWirelength.pdf`. Mechanism: B&B over pairwise relative relations (Moffitt–Pollack lineage) with implied-relation pruning and **incremental network-simplex LP lower bounds** (= C1's subproblem as the inner loop); handles fixed outline, blockages, real pins. Hard evidence: **apte n=9 optimal in 13 s, xerox n=10 in 48 s, hp n=11 in 102 s** (fixed die, 1 thread); synthetic n=20 ≈ 2 h; an IBM 27-rectangle instance solved optimally with tuning; ami33/49 out of reach. Context (area-only packers): Moffitt & Pollack meta-CSP handles **n≤18 under 2 min** (`https://cse.unl.edu/~choueiry/Documents/Moffitt-ICAPS06.pdf`); Korf/Moffitt/Pollack Ann. OR 179:261–295 (2010); Huang & Korf JAIR 46:47–87 (2013), consecutive squares to N=32. Why: our smallest cases (n=7–12 movable after edge-pinning) are *inside the provably-tractable band* — a full exact solve is a real portfolio leg, not fantasy. Sketch: edge blocks as blockages, soft blocks frozen at elite shapes, bundle-weighted center-to-center WL objective, budget-capped leg; exact-score gate on the routed result. Effort L; risk medium-high (routed WL/overflow unmodeled; budget-shared). **NOVEL in this context** (no exact-placement item in the corpus beyond the evidence-free "MILP polish" one-liner).

**C3. LNS polish: exact re-placement of local block groups** — *same paper §6.4 (BonnMacro)*: production flow "repeatedly picks local groups of circuits and re-places them WL-optimally with the exact solver, treating all other circuits as blockages" — 13 s (ami33) / 73 s (ami49), "in regular use … at IBM". General destroy-repair theory: Pisinger & Ropke LNS chapter `https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/files/5293785/Pisinger.pdf`. Why: **α·WL + OA** endgame polish; k≈4–6 blocks re-placed exactly is minutes-cheap at our scale. Sketch: after SA, pick the k blocks with highest bundle-weighted WL contribution, enumerate relative relations (mini-B&B, 4^C(k,2) with pruning) with others fixed, exact-score gate, iterate while budget remains. Effort M (own code, no dependency); risk low (strictly gated, anytime). **NOVEL application** (BonnMacro precedent absent from corpus).

**C4. CP-SAT `no_overlap_2d` polish — evidence now gathered, verdict mixed** — OR-Tools discussion #3177 (`https://github.com/or-tools/or-tools` ecosystem): tight orthogonal-packing feasibility instances still slow vs specialized solvers (maintainer: "the no_overlap_2d propagator is poor"), but loose packings of 50 rectangles solve "almost immediately" (`http://yetanothermathprogrammingconsultant.blogspot.com/2021/02/2d-bin-packing-with-google-or-tools-cp.html`, CP-SAT primer `https://github.com/d-krupke/cpsat-primer`). **No published CP-SAT floorplanning-with-wirelength benchmark exists** — the P5 one-liner stays unproven either way. Decisive local constraint: submission binaries must be static (glibc 2.28, memory: submission-binaries-must-be-static) — static OR-Tools is heavy/risky vs the self-written mini-B&B (C2/C3). Verdict: prefer C2/C3; keep the Sutanthavibul/Shragowitz (DAC 1990 / TCAD 1991, `https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/123186.123255`) soft-block linearization only as formulation material. **PRIOR, now evidenced; not recommended as-is.**

**C5. Alignment/abutment arcs in the redistribution solve** — *Tang & Wong, "Floorplanning with alignment and performance constraints", DAC 2002, DOI 10.1145/513918.514128*; C1's TCAD 2006 formulation supports alignment/abutment natively. Mechanism: alignment as extra constraint-graph arcs evaluated inside the same longest-path/flow solve — no new subsystem. Why: **α·WL + overflow** — *enforces* for the top-k heaviest bundles what the built abutment credit only rewards; straight facing-edge chains have fewer charged crossings. Sketch: add soft alignment arcs (facing-edge midpoint equality with slack) for top-k bundles to the respace graphs; solve C1's flow or the existing relaxation; exact-score gate. Effort S–M; risk low. **PRIOR mechanism, NOVEL composition** with routed-WL gating.

**C6. Bus-driven floorplanning corrections** — canonical BDF is *Xiang, Tang, Wong, ICCAD 2003 / IEEE TCAD 23(11):1522–1530 (2004)* (SP feasibility conditions + SA penalty); the corpus's "Chen & Chang B\*-tree bus-driven TCAD" could not be found — **misattribution, corrected here**. Full BDF (hard strip constraints) is over-constrained for our soft-cost setting; mine only B4's feasibility-bit idea and C5's arcs. **PRIOR.**

## 5. Thread D — production code mining (OpenROAD/Parquet/Corblivar) & SA-meta delta

**Negative findings**: no public ICCAD 2026 Problem E repos as of 2026-08-13 (GitHub API sweeps: `"iccad 2026"` → 19 repos, all Problems A–D/FloorSet; `"problem E" floorplan`, `early floorplanning`, `feedthrough floorplan`, `bus-driven floorplanning` → 0 mineable); adjacent contests (ICCAD 2024/2025, ISPD 2026) are all off-topic (logic opt, MBFF, trojan, buffering). SA-meta 2024–2026 delta beyond the items below is thin; notable production datapoint: **OpenROAD mpl uses NO adaptive cooling/restart/reheat** — fixed 2000-step exponential decay + a diversified 10-run portfolio.

**D1. OpenROAD warmup-average cost normalization + init-prob temperature** — `SimulatedAnnealingCore.cpp`/`SACoreHardMacro.cpp`, BSD-3 (`https://github.com/The-OpenROAD-Project/OpenROAD/blob/master/src/mpl/src/SimulatedAnnealingCore.cpp`). Mechanism: 500 warmup perturbations record each raw cost term; `norm_X = avg`; cost = Σ `w_i·raw_i/norm_i`; `T0 = −avg(Δcost)/log(0.9)`; cooling `T *= exp(log(1e-10/T0)/2000)`. Why: our cheap cost mixes raw units (µm² + α·HPWL + 1e9 penalties); normalization makes one schedule meaningful across 7-vs-24-block cases → acceptance quality → WL. Sketch: per-leg warmup, normalize SA-internal terms only (selection/reported cost unchanged). Effort M; risk: changes acceptance dynamics — seed-sweep revalidation. **PRIOR-variant** (idea classic; production constants new).

**D2. Weight- and utilization-diversified portfolio legs** — `hier_rtlmp.cpp` (`placeMacros`/`placeChildren`): per run `outline_w *= (run_id+1)·10; wl_w /= (run_id+1)` (a monotone outline-hard/WL-soft ladder across 10 runs); cluster placement sweeps target utilization geometrically, inflating soft-macro areas, first valid run wins. README base weights: area 0.1, outline 100, WL 100, boundary 50, notch 10 (`https://github.com/The-OpenROAD-Project/OpenROAD/blob/master/src/mpl/README.md`). Why: our 8 legs diversify by engine/variant/seed only — an **internal-WL-weight ladder** (legs at ×2/×4 internal α·WL) explores placements the current weighting never accepts, targeting the dominant WL gap; a soft-area-inflation leg (~1.1–1.2×) targets overflow/FT. Selection stays by true exact cost, so downside is capped at leg budget. Effort S; risk low. **PRIOR-variant, new axis** for the built portfolio.

**D3. Soft-block "resize-to-neighbor-edge" move** — `SACoreSoftMacro.cpp` `resizeOneCluster` (move mix: 4 swaps ×0.2 + resize 0.4; resize = 25% each snap width/height up/down to the nearest neighbor block edge, else random). Why: deterministic abutment closes dead pockets and straightens channels → **WL + overflow**, vs our blind AR sampling. Sketch: new mutation in both engines using the last decoded `Placement`: set w or h to abut the nearest neighbor edge, clamped to AR range. Effort M; risk low (stale geometry is still validity-checked). **NOVEL** (absent from corpus).

**D4. Notch penalty with concrete thresholds** — `SACoreSoftMacro.cpp` `calNotchPenalty`: thresholds `outline/10`; boolean occupancy grid; flood-fill empty regions; penalty per notch `sqrt(w·h/outline_area)`, weight 10 vs WL 100. Complements A5 (MAGE metrics). Effort M; **PRIOR-variant** (concept known via Hier-RTLMP paper; algorithm + constants extracted now).

**D5. ParquetFP slack-directed move — exact production recipe** — `ParquetFP/src/btreeanneal.cxx` @ OpenROAD `d05b787` (`https://github.com/The-OpenROAD-Project/OpenROAD/blob/d05b787d51f0fd58d529aaaf8e6f974fbe4e2169/src/mpl/src/ParquetFP/src/btreeanneal.cxx`; theory Adya–Markov TVLSI'03 `https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~imarkov/pubs/jour/tvlsi03-fixedFP.pdf`). Mechanism: compute per-block x/y slacks from the two longest-path passes; operand = random pick among the lowest ⌈N/5⌉ slack blocks, target = among the highest ⌈N/5⌉; re-insert operand next to target; AR-violation-directed variant force-triggered every 4th temperature step; fixed-outline delta hard-coded `0.2·ΔHPWL + 0.4·ΔAR + 0.4·ΔwastedArea` with pre-normalized deltas. Why: relocates exactly the blocks pinning the bounding box → OA + acceptance of WL-improving compaction. Sketch: SP engine already computes longest paths — add the slack move at ~10–15% weight; B\*: transplant node under target. Effort M; risk medium (insertion placement is representation-specific). **PRIOR-variant** (IAR/slack moves long known-unbuilt; this is the exact selection/threshold recipe from production code).

**D6. Corblivar feasibility-ratio adaptive weights + stagnation reheat** — `DfX-NYUAD/Corblivar` `src/FloorPlanner.cpp` (**GPL-3.0 — reimplement mechanism only**, documented in Knechtel's papers). Mechanism: per temperature step, `ratio = fitting_layouts/accepted_ops`; `cost_area *= 0.5·w·(1+ratio)`, `cost_outline *= 0.5·w·(1−ratio)` (weight flows from fit to quality as feasibility rises); phase-3 **reheat** when stddev of the last 3 per-step average costs ≤ 1e-4; T0 = stddev(cost samples)·factor. Why: the only floorplanning production code found doing in-run weight adaptation + reheat — the floorplanning-specific precedent the 07-07 survey lacked for both ideas. Sketch: ratio = fraction of recent accepted states legal & overflow-free; scale internal α·WL by (1+r)/2, penalties by (1−r)/2 per exact-interval epoch; stagnation-triggered `T *= k`. Effort M; risk: oscillation — freeze ratio per epoch. **PRIOR-variant** (floorplanning-specific trigger/constants new).
